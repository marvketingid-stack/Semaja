Long before the first plate is served, a restaurant makes a promise with its room. At Semaja, that promise is quiet, warm, and unmistakably Indonesian.

## Materials with memory

Natural textures do the talking here — hand-woven wall art, aged wood, and rattan that softens every light source. Nothing shouts. Each material is chosen to age gracefully and to complement the food rather than compete with it.

> "The room should feel like a home you have never lived in, yet somehow remember." — on the design of Semaja

## Light as an ingredient

Warm, low lighting turns an ordinary evening into an occasion. Shadows fall gently across batik-patterned walls, and the glow of rattan lamps invites you to slow down — exactly the pace a Nusantara feast deserves.

## Three ways to gather

From the **Main Dining Hall** for a lively dinner, to the calm of the **Indoor Garden**, to the privacy of the **VIP Room**, each space is designed for a different kind of evening. Whichever you choose, the story stays the same: heritage, warmth, and thoughtful detail.

Come see the room for yourself — and stay for the table.
