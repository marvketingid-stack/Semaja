At Semaja, every plate is a conversation between past and present. We believe Indonesia's culinary wealth is far too vast to forget — from **Sate Maranggi** in the Sundanese highlands to the smoked spices of the eastern islands.

## A living heritage

The recipes we serve are not mere replicas. Chef Glenn Erari traces the origin of each dish, then reimagines it with modern technique: controlled smoking, clean plating, and a precise balance of flavour.

> "Flavour is memory. Our job is to keep it alive, not to freeze it." — Chef Glenn Erari

## From the regions to Menteng

What makes Semaja special is the courage to bring rarely-spotlighted regional dishes to a fine dining table in the heart of Jakarta. A warm room of wood and batik completes the experience.

Come and taste it for yourself — the best stories always begin at the dinner table.
